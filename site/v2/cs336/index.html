CS336: Language Modeling from Scratch

2026-10-06 · Instructor: Prof. Christopher Manning

CS336 index , learner navigation

Start here

  1. Read README.md for the source-grounding note and honesty rules.
  2. Take the diagnostic in prerequisites.md. Follow the remediation links for any item you cannot answer.
  3. Read notation_and_shapes.md once. Keep it open while you study.
  4. Work units in order: U01 through U18. Each unit lists its prerequisites and its local remediation block.
  5. Consolidate: the two capstones in capstones/, the transfer sets and oral defenses in interview/, then crash-course.md and cheatsheet.md for review.

Study protocol

For each concept: read the lesson contract items 1 through 12, attempt item 13 closed-book, check the key, then do the lab tasks. Log confident mistakes in mastery_ledger.md. Revisit on a spaced schedule.
