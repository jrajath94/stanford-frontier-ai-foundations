Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning

Course home for math-ml: a deep crash course on the mathematics behind machine learning. The course follows the NPTEL/IISc course noc26-cs02, “Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning”, by Prof. Prathosh A P (IISc 2021-2026, IIT Delhi 2017-2021). Twelve weeks. The first offering ran Jan 19, 2026 to Apr 10, 2026, with the exam on Apr 25, 2026. The second offering began in August 2026.

Recordings live in the YouTube playlist PLgMDNELGJ1Cay-Q9Cn8KcpUcC58NDWuiu. The playlist was enumerated on 2026-10-06: 89 entries (1 intro, 69 lectures Lec 01-Lec 69, 19 tutorials).

The ten units

Unit Topic Lesson pages
U01 Mathematical language and computational setup: sets, functions, notation, units, sums, logs, vectors, honest plots, assertions, precision, reproducibility Lesson 01, keys, Interactive companion
U02 Linear geometry and decompositions: subspaces, rank, projections, eigenvectors, SVD, covariance, conditioning Lesson 02, keys, Interactive companion
U03 Calculus and optimization: partials, chain rule, convexity, gradient descent, Newton, constraints, KKT, learning rates Lesson 03, keys, Interactive companion
U04 Probability, density, and estimation, in three source blocks: sample spaces and variables (a), entropy/KL/MLE (b), risk framework, Bayes, EM, nonparametric methods (c) Block a, Block b, Block c, keys a, keys b, keys c, Interactive companion
U05 Risk, regularization, and generalization: empirical and population risk, bias and variance, penalties, priors, CV, leakage, shift Lesson 05, keys, Interactive companion
U06 Linear models, kernels, and margins: OLS, logistic, softmax, GLMs, kernels, Gram matrices, SVM, the dual Lesson 06, keys, Interactive companion
U07 Trees and ensembles: decision regions, impurity, pruning, bagging, random forests, boosting, imbalance, baselines Lesson 07, keys, Interactive companion
U08 Neural and sequence architectures: MLP, backprop, CNNs, RNNs, gates, attention, transformers, normalization Lesson 08, keys, Interactive companion
U09 Unsupervised and latent models: k-means, PCA, reconstruction, held-out evaluation (the EM and GMM leaves live in the U04c block) Lesson 09b, keys, Interactive companion
U10 Generative bridge and mathematical synthesis: VAEs, GANs, objectives, samples versus densities, research critique, source-gap audit Lesson 10, keys, Interactive companion

How to use this course

  1. Take the placement diagnostic first. It tells you which units to read slowly and which to skim.
  2. Read the units in order. U04 runs as three source blocks (a, b, c). U09 spreads across the U04c block (EM, GMMs, latent models) and the U09b lesson (k-means, PCA).
  3. Answer keys live in separate files, one per lesson. Attempt every exercise before you open the key.
  4. Labs give three computation tasks per unit. The interview bank gives breadth questions, deep ladders, quantitative drills, and oral defenses per unit, with keys separate. Two capstones close the course.
  5. The crash course compresses all ten units into thirty minutes. The cheatsheet holds every formula, shape, and trap on one dense page.
  6. Notation is fixed in notation and shapes. Terms are fixed in the glossary.
