Lab 03 · Keys

2026-10-06 · Instructor: Prof. Prathosh A P

Lab keys 03, calculus and optimization computations

Date: 2026-10-06. Computed values, numpy 1.26.4, float64.

Task 1

(a) w = 39/30 = 1.3. (b) Gradient at w = 1: -2.25. Gradient at w: 4.440892098500626e-16, within 1e-12 of zero. Float dust, not a remainder. (c) New w = 1 - 0.1 * (-2.25) = 1.225. The cost fell: the gradient was negative, so w rose toward w* = 1.3.

Task 2

(a) Final J: eta = 0.05 -> 0.7178979876918524. eta = 0.5 -> 0.0. eta = 1.5 -> 150994944.0. (b) Multipliers: 0.9 (shrinks), 0.0 (lands), 2.0 (doubles the distance each step). Prediction before measuring: slow crawl, one-step landing, explosion. (c) Strong answer: “Your fixed step crossed the stability boundary mid-run: the valley narrowed, the curvature grew, and 1/L fell below your eta. Cut the rate and check whether the divergence step matches the |1 - 2 eta| arithmetic.”

Task 3

(a) Iterates: 1.0, 1.333333, 1.236715, 1.224916, 1.224745, 1.224745. Final value 1.224745. (b) sqrt(1.5) = 1.224744871391589. Match within 1e-9. (c) From x0 = 0.1, Newton converges to 0.0, the saddle. The Hessian at 0.1 is 12*0.01 - 6 = -5.88, negative: the local parabola opens downward, so Newton aims at its peak, the hill, not a bowl. Wrong target, correct arithmetic.
