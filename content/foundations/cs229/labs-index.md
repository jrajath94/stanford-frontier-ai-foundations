Three labs built for frontier-lab ML interviews. Each is a 45-minute timed exercise. Do them with pen and paper first, then check against the notes.

1. [Lab 1: Derivations by hand](lab1-derivations.html). normal equations, logistic regression gradient, backprop for one layer. The three derivations interviewers ask for most.
2. [Lab 2: Bias-variance in practice](lab2-bias-variance.html). diagnose a failing model from its learning curves and prescribe the fix.
3. [Lab 3: EM from scratch](lab3-em-from-scratch.html). implement Gaussian mixture clustering in NumPy.

## Rules

- 45 minutes per lab, no notes, no tools for Lab 1.
- For Lab 2 and 3 you may use Python/NumPy.
- If you get stuck for more than 10 minutes on one step, read the hint, then redo the step from scratch.
