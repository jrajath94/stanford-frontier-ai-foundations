## Week structure corroboration (SRC-06, secondary)

Week 1 ERM intro. Week 2 Bayes and divergences. Week 3 MLE/MAP and
nonparametric. Week 4 linear models. Week 5 regularization. Week 6 kernels
and SVM. Week 7 perceptron, NNs, backprop. Week 8 CNNs. Week 9 sequences. Week 10 attention and transformers. Week 11 trees and ensembles. Week 12 unsupervised, EM, generative preview.
