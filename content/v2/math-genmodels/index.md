One question drives every lesson: you have samples from an
unknown rule. Learn the rule. Then draw fresh samples from it.
Each family of generative models answers that question
differently, and each answer has its own mathematics, its own
hand-worked toys, and its own honest price.

This course is the companion to the sibling math-genai course,
which covers the mathematical foundations (KL, divergences, ELBO,
GAN mathematics). Here the families take center stage: the
storyteller, the sculptor, the warper, the restorer, the critic,
and the judge who scores them all.

## Lessons

1. [L01 · The One Question](l01-the-one-question.html): the distribution, the exploding table, the five machines.
2. [L02 · Autoregressive Models](l02-autoregressive-models.html): chain rule by hand, teacher forcing, exposure bias, the serial price.
3. [L03 · Variational Autoencoders](l03-variational-autoencoders.html): the reparameterization trick with real numbers, the KL rent, why samples blur.
4. [L04 · Normalizing Flows](l04-normalizing-flows.html): change of variables, the Jacobian determinant on toys, coupling layers, rigidity.
5. [L05 · Diffusion I: DDPM](l05-diffusion-ddpm.html): the forward step by hand, noise-prediction training, the 1,000-step sampling loop.
6. [L06 · Score Matching and Diffusion II](l06-score-matching-ddim.html): the score by hand, denoising score matching, DDIM shortcuts, guidance.
7. [L07 · Energy-Based Models](l07-energy-based-models.html): energies, the partition-function bill, contrastive divergence by hand.
8. [L08 · Judging a Creator](l08-judging-a-creator.html): likelihood, FID on 2-D Gaussians, Inception Score, precision/recall, every blind spot demonstrated.
